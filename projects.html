Almost Human

Almost Human is a game I am currently working on in Unity in my spare time.
It will be a 3D first person horror game with puzzle elements where you explore an abandoned secret
research facility in a post-apocalyptic America.
Uncover the truth in this Residnet Evil inspired game. Early WIP.

Turret Game

Turret Game is a simple game I made in Unity where you control a turret to shoot incoming enemies.
The goal is to survive as many rounds as possible against different enemy variants.
You will be able to upgrade your turret at the shop between rounds to help you survive longer. Early WIP.

Water Game

This is a really early in development 2D platformer game. You play as a water droplet that can jump and shoot water.
Shoot water to defeat enemies but beware! Shooting water causes you to shrink and lose health.
You can collect water to restore your health and size. Early WIP.
